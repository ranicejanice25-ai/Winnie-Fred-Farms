# Winnie Fred Farms System



## Background of the Organization



The Winnie-Fred brand is a family run farm company named after the matriarch and patriarch of the family. They are operating out of Crescent, Horsefording Bonny Gate, St. Mary.



They are a diversified agricultural farm dedicated to producing crops, particularly Irish potatoes, pak choy, and bee products with hopes to expand operations by incorporating livestock alongside crop production.



They are currently managing their honey orders primarily via a rudimentary excel sheet, however have no system for tracking or generating invoices or receipts.



## The Problem/Need



The simple excel sheet that the company is currently using is inflexible and lacks the full suite of features necessary to run a business, especially as clientele grows. Only one person currently has access to this sheet, which is only tracking one product. As such a software system is needed to help manage this family business operations.
